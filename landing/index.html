Crafted for Developers

Everything you need to master your Git repos

Instant Local Discovery

Point Gitler at your Desktop, Developer, or Projects folder. It scans recursively in milliseconds, identifying uncommitted changes, branches, and remote sync states.

On-Device Commit Suggestions

Draft Conventional Commit messages from staged diffs with local Ollama or LM Studio models. Built-in suggestions work without a model. Review every message before you commit.

Self-Hosted & Local Network Git

Support for Gitea, Forgejo, GitLab, and GitHub. Includes dedicated auto-generated SSH keys, auto-sync, and native macOS Local Network Privacy compliance.

Warp Dev Tools Aesthetic

Flat Obsidian palettes, high-contrast typography, pill buttons, and draggable panel dividers. Switches between Dark and Paper Light themes.

Zero Cloud Telemetry

Gitler does not connect to any analytics or cloud databases. Your code, secrets, commit history, and diffs remain strictly on your local disk.

Single Standalone macOS .dmg

Download the Developer ID signed installer for Apple Silicon. Drag Gitler to Applications and keep your repositories in one workspace.

100% Free & Open Source

Built for the open source community under the MIT license. Fork it, customize the AI prompt templates, or contribute features on GitHub.

Visual Diffs & File Staging

Review changes before you commit. Stage or unstage individual files, and inspect separate previews for staged changes and your working tree.

Branches & Commit History

Create and switch branches without leaving your workspace. Browse recent commits, check remote sync status, and fetch, pull, or push when you are ready.

Under the Hood

Commit suggestions, kept on your Mac

Start with built-in suggestions, or connect a local AI model. Your staged changes stay on your machine, and you stay in control of the final commit.
