Crafted for Developers

Everything you need to master your Git repos

Instant Local Discovery

Choose your Projects folder and see your repositories together. Find uncommitted changes, check branches, and spot projects that need a push or pull.

Visual Diffs & File Staging

Review changes before you commit. Stage or unstage individual files, and inspect separate previews for staged changes and your working tree.

Branches & Commit History

Create and switch branches without leaving your workspace. Browse recent commits, check remote sync status, and fetch, pull, or push when you are ready.

AI Copilot for Your Repos

Ask questions about your code, understand changes, and draft commit messages with a local model. Keep the final decisions in your hands.

Zero Cloud Telemetry

Git Mate does not connect to any analytics or cloud databases. Your code, secrets, commit history, and diffs remain strictly on your local disk.

100% Free & Open Source

Use the full workspace without a subscription. Explore the source, adapt it to your workflow, or contribute on GitHub under the MIT license.
